You did it. You got through 2020.

It's been a strange, difficult year for pretty much everyone. But amongst all the chaos, there were still good things — small moments of joy, connection, and gratitude that are worth remembering.

Here are some of the nice things that happened and the things I'm grateful for this year:

- **Family and friends** — Even when we couldn't be together in person, the people who mattered most found ways to stay close.
- **Slowing down** — Forced pauses gave space to reflect, read, walk, and think about what actually matters.
- **Small wins** — Finishing uni remotely, keeping this blog going, and learning to appreciate the little things.

Here's to a better year ahead. Thank you for reading along.
